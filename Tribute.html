Biography



Peter Parker was an orphaned teenage boy who lived in Queens,
New York with his Aunt May and Uncle Ben. He was a shy boy,
but highly intelligent and excelled in science. The spider bite
gave Peter spider-like powers with super strength and reflexes.
He also had a “spider-sense” alerting him to danger. He was
often teased by other more popular kids like longtime nemesis
Flash Thompson, but his life was soon to change on a visit to the
science museum.

"with great power comes great responsibility"
